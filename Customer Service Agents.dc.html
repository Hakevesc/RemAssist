Do we have to change our helpdesk software?

No. Agents work inside the tool you already run — Zendesk, Intercom, GoHighLevel, HubSpot or something in-house — using your macros and tags. If we part ways, your data and audit trail stay exactly where they are; there is nothing to migrate back.

How do you cover nights and weekends without overtime?

With a rota rather than a longer day. Extended hours means two or more seats sharing coverage; true 24/7 means a pod of three to six across timezones. That is why coverage drives cost far more than seniority does.

What does “QA-reviewed” actually mean?

Every interaction is scored against criteria you agree, by a person who owns your account. You get a daily work report and a weekly quality summary, and the scoring is visible to you rather than summarised into one number.

How do you handle data security?

Every seat operates under independently audited ISO 9001 (quality) and ISO 27001 (information security) controls, with access scoped per client and reviewed when people join or leave your account. Because agents work in your helpdesk, your data stays in your systems rather than being copied into ours.

What if the agents are not a good fit?

You approve every agent before they answer a single ticket, and you can ask us to replace a seat that is not working. The first 30–60 days run as a pilot at reduced rates, so you are judging the work rather than the pitch — and there is no long-term lock-in either way. The consultation itself is always free.

Can agents learn a technical product?

Yes, and product training is completed before the first ticket. For accounts that need deeper knowledge we place from a dedicated niche track rather than the generalist bench, and we will tell you on the call which one fits.
